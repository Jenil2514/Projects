About Company

Pioneering Cleaning Science & B2B Hygiene Manufacturing

Established in Vadodara, Gujarat, Shreeji Chemical has emerged as a premier manufacturer of consumer and commercial cleaning products. Under our trusted brand AIKA, we produce laboratory-tested, dermatologically safe home cleaning liquids, power laundry detergents, antibacterial handwashes, toilet cleaners, and heavy-duty floor disinfectants.

Equipped with state-of-the-art automated liquid blending facilities, rigorous quality assurance standards, and an experienced R&D team, we cater to both household consumers and large commercial buyers, hotels, hospitals, and institutional facilities.
