ABOUT JHG ARCHITECTURE

CRAFTING TIMELSS ARCHITECTURAL LANDMARKS

Founded in New York City, JHG is a multidisciplinary architecture and interior design studio dedicated to creating extraordinary residential, commercial, and urban environments.

We blend modernist rigor with tactile material warmth. Every project is conceived as a unique dialogue between site topography, light play, structural innovation, and the client's vision.
