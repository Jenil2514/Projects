Everything You Need to Know About Your Visit

Transparent answers about our clinical safety, diagnostic turnaround, E-Prescriptions, and insurance coverage.

We maintain a rigorous zero-contamination clinical protocol. Every private consultation suite undergoes surgical-grade UV-C light surface sterilization and HEPA 14 air filtration between patient visits. All instruments are single-use or medical autoclave-certified, achieving a 0% hospital-acquired infection rate.

Your appointment is strictly unhurried. Your board-certified physician will perform a comprehensive evaluation, review your symptoms and medical history in detail, conduct necessary biometric diagnostics, and answer all your questions before co-creating your personalized care plan.

Most routine blood panels and DICOM radiograph X-ray scans are processed on the same day. As soon as your attending radiologist signs off, your full report is securely uploaded to your digital Patient Portal and accessible on your phone.

Yes. Your doctor digitally signs your E-Prescription with a secure cryptographic signature. It is instantly transmitted electronically to your designated pharmacy (CVS, Walgreens, or direct clinic pickup) so your medications are ready when you arrive.

We accept all major health insurance providers (Blue Cross, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare, etc.) for both in-clinic and virtual telehealth sessions. We also offer transparent, zero-hidden-fee self-pay rates prior to booking.

You can log into your secure Patient Portal 24/7 to download encounter SOAP notes, track blood pressure and vital trends over time, view active medications, or message your physician directly.
